평가 2023 / 순서 4 / 2022-10-03 / Fipronil
목적·대상: Risk assessment / Plant products / 원래 판정: No
출처: https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2022.7521#efs27521-tbl-0053
원문 검증: Repository transcription; full 2022 article retrieval was unsuccessful. Primary web index also exposes the Thiamethoxam comment.
EFSA 원문 코멘트:
Fipronil sulfone metabolite (MB46136) Fipronil desulfinyl metabolite (MB46513) Fipronil sulfide metabolite (MB45950) The enforcement residue definitions derived by the JMPR and the EU peer review/MRL review are comparable. The risk assessment residue definition derived by the JMPR is wider than the EU risk assessment residue definition. It includes the sum of fipronil, the desulfinyl metabolite (MB46513), the sulfone metabolite (MB46136) and the sulfide metabolite (MB45950).
New metabolism studies, investigating the metabolic pathway of fipronil following foliar treatments were evaluated by the JMPR 2021 (cabbage potato, rice, cotton, sunflower) which have not been available for the EU assessments. These studies, however, were not the reason to amend the residue definition for risk assessment as derived by the JMPR in 2002. The studies confirmed the presence of photometabolite of fipronil (desulfinyl metabolite (MB46513)) following foliar treatment.
The sulfide metabolite (MB45950), also included in the risk assessment residue definitions, was present in rice straw (9.3–16.8%) following seed/foliar treatment, but generally not detected or present at low levels in other commodities. The sulfide metabolite (MB45950) was present also in goat matrices (up to 12% TRR).
Overall, the JMPR concluded that metabolites MB46513 and MB45950 could make a significant contribution to the overall consumer exposure when compared with estimates for the sum of fipronil and fipronil-sulfone and therefore included these compounds in the risk assessment residue definition. The EU pesticides peer review derived the risk assessment and enforcement residue definitions for the representative use, i.e.
seed treatment; the photodegradation product of fipronil-desulfinyl metabolite (MB46513) was not included in the residue definitions as unlikely to occur in seed treatments or soil incorporation. However, because of concern that the desulfinyl metabolite forms as a result of photodegradation, it was proposed that treated seed must be kept in the dark prior to use. The MRL review confirmed the residue definitions derived by the EU pesticides peer review.
The risk assessment residue definition derived by the JMPR is adequate to account for the potential metabolites of fipronil in plant commodities (from seed/soil and foliar applications) and in animal matrices. The exposure assessment performed according to this residue definition is expected to be protective for consumers. The JMPR also evaluated hydrolysis studies submitted to investigate the effect of processing on the nature of fipronil, MB46136 and MB46153.
Fipronil under sterilisation conditions slightly degraded for 5.5% AR to fipronil-amide (toxicity covered by fipronil). Fipronil sulfone (MB46136) also degraded under sterilisation conditions only and for 8.4% AR to RPA105320 (fipronil-sulfone-amide). Fipronil-desulfinyl was the most unstable and under sterilisation conditions formed two major degradation products: MB46400 (10.5% AR) and fipronil-carboxamide (25.9% AR; RPA105048).
For the estimation of consumer exposure to these degradation products the JMPR proposed to apply the TTC (Cramer Class III threshold of 1.5 μg/kg bw per day).
모델에 추가할 영어 요약:
EFSA states that the JMPR risk-assessment definition is broader than the EU definition. It additionally accounts for MB46513 and MB45950, which can contribute to exposure beyond fipronil and its sulfone metabolite.
한국어 설명:
EFSA는 JMPR 위해평가 정의가 EU보다 넓고, fipronil과 sulfone 이외의 노출에 기여할 수 있는 MB46513과 MB45950도 반영한다고 설명한다.
주의:
집행용은 비교 가능하다는 같은 코멘트의 별도 문장과 혼동하지 않는다.
